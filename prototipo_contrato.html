What no register gives

The date of the tender (only the notice number), the reason for the stoppage, the date the extension was signed, the month of each note (São Paulo publishes the year only) and the disbursement of the loan matched to the note. These are the blanks the method keeps as blanks.

1What the work is

The object text is read once, by rule: the highway code (SP-095), the kilometre marks (43.7 to 74.4, 30.7 km) and the class of work from the verbs. "Duplicação" puts it in capacity expansion; "restauração" alone would be maintenance.

Fields: rodovia SP-095, extensao_km 30.7, class capacity expansion.

2Procurement stage

The notice number gives the year of the tender (2012) and the modality (CO, concorrência). No register publishes the date of the notice, so the procurement stage is measured in years, not months, and only where a notice exists (SP, MG, RS).

Fields: ano_edital 2012, modality concorrência; tender date absent.

3Linking the notes to the contract

The notes carry no contract number. The process number on every note (A26109712) is matched to the notice of the record in the DER register; the link is one to one. Method "process × notice", status probable, 99.0% accuracy in the blind validation on 2022-2024, where the DER publishes the note-contract sheet.

Result: 22 note-years linked; link strength "confirmed or probable".

4Channel by the paying source (rule P-041)

Each note takes the channel of its budget source. Sources 001, 041, 004, 006 and 042 are the state's own money: channel 1a. Source 007 is labelled "credit operations and contributions from abroad", but the notes name the operation, DV0001075 and DV0001079, a Banco do Brasil loan: the named operation wins, channel 3 domestic credit, not 4. The Treasury notes of 2013 and 2014, same contract and same year as the loan, are the counterpart: they stay in 1a with a counterpart mark and are never moved to the loan's channel.

Result: two channels (1a 59.7%, 3 40.3% of the paid value); family "Treasury + credit", a loan with its counterpart; multichannel = yes.

5Stages side by side, never added

Committed, settled, paid in the year and carry-over paid are four columns. Note 2012NE03725 is committed for 12.34 in 2012, pays nothing that year, and is paid 9.60 in 2013 and 2.74 in 2014 as carry-over. The reconciliation is the register's total: 47.71 paid in the year plus 14.07 carry-over equals 61.78, the accumulated total of the record.

Indicator: carry-over 22.8% of what was paid (problem 4 threshold is 25%: not flagged).

6Term and extension

Initial term 18 months, current term 83: extended by 65 months, 3.6 times the original term. The extension is read from the two term fields of the register, so it exists only where a register publishes both (SP, BA, SC, part of MG and RS).

Indicator: extended = yes; 12 months or more = yes; 50% of the term or more = yes (problem 1).

7Interruption

Two readings that do not coincide. The register records a stoppage from 26 Feb 2016 to 25 Jul 2017, 17 months, inside the fiscal adjustment years; only SP, SC and RJ record stoppages. The notes show a payment in every year from 2012 to 2019, so the payment-gap rule, a year without any payment, finds no gap, even though 2017 paid R$ 1.8 thousand. The two measures are kept apart and never compared across spheres.

Indicators: stoppage recorded = yes (17 months); payment gap = no.

8Value

Value with amendments equals the initial value: no value amendment. The accumulated total exceeds it by the price adjustment only (3.40 on 58.39). Growth is read as current value above the original by more than 0.1%, so this contract is not flagged.

Indicator: value growth = no (problem 2); price adjustment 5.8%.

9What enters the base, and the verdict

One row in contratos_pareados_14 with the fields above. In the clean-finish funnel the contract passes "ended by 2025", "no interruption" (by the payment rule) and "not rescinded" (São Paulo has no status field, so the step removes nothing there), and fails "not extended". Audit regimes: the state court over the whole contract and the lender's rules over the loan share, two regimes.

Verdict: finished at 99.99% of its value, 65 months late, after a 17-month stoppage, paid by two channels; a typical large Treasury-and-credit work of the base.
