What no register gives

The date of the tender, the reason for the 210 days stalled, the dates of the 22 amendments, and every payment before 2021: the works started in November 2014, but no note before 2021 carries the contract number, so the first six years of payment cannot be linked to this contract. Three registers give three situations for the same work.

1What the work is

The object text gives the highway (BR-116/BA), the kilometre marks (387.41 to 427.75, 40.34 km) and the service: duplication with side roads and bridges, under the integrated regime (the contractor designs and builds). ObrasGov splits the contract into two works, the Ibó–Feira upgrade and the Feira de Santana ring road, which the method keeps under one contract.

Fields: highway BR-116, 40.34 km, class capacity expansion, design and build = yes, two works.

2Procurement stage

The modality is known (RDC, the differentiated regime of Law 12.462/2011, procedure 292/2014-05) because the contract record names it; three quarters of the federal contracts have it. No federal register publishes the date of the tender notice, so the procurement stage has no duration on the Union side.

Fields: modality RDC; tender date absent.

3Linking the notes to the contract

The link comes from ObrasGov, which lists the notes of each work, and is confirmed by the text of every note ("05.1.0.00.0879.2014, RDC 292/2014-05") and by the two internal plans. It exists from 2021 only: the Union's notes carry a contract reference from 2014 for a few contracts and from 2020-2021 for most, which is why the federal contract layer of the base starts in 2020 and why this contract's payments of 2014-2020 stay unlinked.

Result: 18 notes, 2021-2024, link confirmed; 2014-2020 not linkable.

4Channel by the paying source

Every note is Union budget paid by the DNIT: channel 1a, direct execution. Within it the sources are free Treasury resources (79%), Treasury bonds (20%, the 2023 notes) and a sliver of earmarked revenue; two notes carry a state-caucus amendment of the Bahia bench. At the contract level this is a single channel. The mix is on the asset: the BR-116 in Bahia carried Union transfers until 2008, the ViaBahia concession from 2009, DNIT direct execution throughout, and the state's own contracts on the access roads. The ten federal contracts with a loan tranche are old (2002-2005) and small.

Result: contract single channel (1a); asset two channels every year since 2009 (1a and 5) plus state money on the feeders.

5Stages side by side, never added

Committed 297.31, paid in the year 183.99, carry-over paid 97.69. Six notes of December 2022 and 2023 (35.00, 16.98, 10.00, 4.61, 3.94, 1.55) were committed at year end and paid entirely as carry-over the next year; note 2022NE000485 was committed and paid in full in 2022.

Indicator: carry-over 34.7% of what was paid (problem 4, threshold 25%: flagged).

6Term and extension

Signed in October 2014 with the end planned for October 2018 (48 months); the term now runs to April 2025, 78 months later, after 22 amendments that added 2,116 days. The federal extension is read from the planned and current end of the ObrasGov record; the Painel de Contratos gives the days added.

Indicator: extended = yes; 12 months or more = yes; above 50% of the term = yes (problem 1).

7Interruption

The Painel de Contratos records 210 days stalled; ObrasGov marks one of the two works as unfinished with 43% physical progress. On the federal side interruption is a recorded status, not a payment gap: the notes start in 2021, so a year without payment cannot be read for the whole life of the contract. This is why the federal 36.8% and the state 5.4% of problem 5 are never compared.

Indicators: days stalled = 210; unfinished = yes; payment gap = not measurable.

8Value

Three readings of the same contract. The current value (265.31) is below the initial value (297.00): by the rule, no value growth. The Painel adds 201.86 of price readjustment and reaches 467.17, of which 263.08 is measured (56%). The SUPRA snapshot of December 2023 shows 529.66 in force and 418.46 measured (79%). The method keeps the first as the indicator and the other two as columns, never mixed.

Indicator: value growth = no (problem 2); readjustment 68% of the initial value.

9What enters the base, and the verdict

One row of the federal project base. In the clean-finish funnel the contract passes "ended by 2025" (term end April 2025) and then meets the federal register gap at step 2: the Painel says concluded, the DNIT register says awaiting completion, ObrasGov says completed and unfinished. The method keeps it out of "completed" until the ObrasGov status is validated, which is the 71% drop of the federal line in Chart 24. Audit: TCU and CGU over the whole contract; no lender.

Verdict: a 40 km duplication under the integrated regime, 78 months late, 22 amendments, a third of the payment as carry-over, three statuses and no linkable payment before 2021; the typical large federal work of the base.
